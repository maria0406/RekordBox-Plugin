What "connect" means here

This app runs under one developer's Spotify app, which Spotify's Development Mode caps at 5 allowlisted testers. You don't need to create your own developer account — the app owner adds your Spotify account to that list once. If you can see this page, you're already allowlisted; clicking below just logs you in and asks you to approve the permissions this app needs (your profile, reading your playlists, and creating a new private playlist for the ordered output). Nothing is posted or changed in your account until you explicitly ask it to at the results stage.
